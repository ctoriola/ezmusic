About Us

Doing the Kingdom Business, One Artiste at a Time

EeZee Conceptz Limited is registered with the Corporate Affairs Commission of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Our vision is to reach our generation and win souls for Christ through music, creating a positive platform for talented gospel artistes, blessing lives with inspired lyrics and ministrations, and discouraging social vices and violence.

Our Target
Our target is the youth of society, for the glory of the youth is in their strength. We channel this potential rightly through the positive impartation of God’s Word and power.
